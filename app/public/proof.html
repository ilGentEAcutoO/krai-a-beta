Known limits

What the numbers above don't tell you yet, or where the system still falls short.

Hang-up

An unusually phrased demand closes about 3 s later

A hard demand the T1 code rules recognise (OTP, app install, transfer now) closes the call at once. A demand phrased in a way T1 does not catch still hears the receptionist's short question first, and Spark closes the call about 3 s later.

Test voices

Real-audio callers are TTS, not people

10 calls (5 Thai, 5 English) streamed in real time at phone quality. The recorded callers don't wait for the next question; a live caller usually would.

Data

The sealed sets are small and mostly Thai

The sealed sets were never used for few-shot, cache seeds or threshold tuning, but v1 (35 calls) is all Thai and v2 (60 calls) is 51 Thai, 9 English. Small n: 34/34 scams caught has a 95% CI of 89.8–100%. Other languages come from the train set and the AI vs AI arena.

Cost

AI cost counts the judge only

Voice and telephony are not included. Tuning runs sent 8 calls at once, so their latency reads a little high.
